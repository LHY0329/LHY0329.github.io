I am a Ph.D. student in Instrument Science and Technology at Hefei University of Technology. My research focuses on magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) reconstruction, particularly on developing reliable, data-efficient, and generalizable reconstruction methods for accelerated and high-dimensional MRI.

My current research explores learning paradigms that move beyond fully supervised reconstruction, with an emphasis on unsupervised and semi-supervised methods. I am also interested in uncertainty quantification, model generalization across different datasets and acquisition settings, and reconstruction methods for dynamic and high-dimensional MRI, including 4D and 5D imaging.

## Research Interests

- Accelerated and parallel MRI reconstruction
- Unsupervised and semi-supervised reconstruction
- Uncertainty quantification and reconstruction reliability
- Model generalization and robustness
- Dynamic and high-dimensional MRI reconstruction
- 4D and 5D medical imaging

My previous work has investigated variational networks, sensitivity-map refinement, and deep learning architectures for accelerated parallel MRI reconstruction. More information about my research is available on the [Publications](/publications/) page.
